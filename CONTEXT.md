# Aquarium

Players raise fish, decorate their tanks, and choose which fish to sell or retain.

## Language

**Fish**:
An animal owned by the player. All fish share the same growth, care and sale rules, whether their eggs cost coins or pearls.
_Avoid_: Companion, growing fish and display fish as ownership categories

**Favorite**:
A fish the player has marked to protect it from selling. It remains in the tank and uses the same space as any other fish.

**Tank capacity**:
The number of fish and eggs that can be placed in a tank. All fish share this capacity.
_Avoid_: Growing slots, display slots, showcase slots

**Sale**:
The exchange of an eligible fish for its current coin and XP value. Retaining or favoriting a fish is not a sale and gives no sale reward.
_Avoid_: Keep as a reward action, rehome
