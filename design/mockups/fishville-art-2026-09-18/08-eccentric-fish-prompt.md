# Eccentric fish with clear fish anatomy

Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: cartoon game fish concept sheet.
Primary request: Restore the strange, eccentric, slightly grotesque cartoon character and HUGE bulging eyes of the earlier art, while keeping the anatomy unmistakably fishlike. The last revision became too ordinary and restrained. The user wants weird, memorable fish with big eyes, not realistic or generic cute aquarium fish.

Input images:
1. Earlier cartoon character study: PRIMARY reference for the offbeat character design, enormous cream-colored eyeballs, tiny pupils, awkward expressions and simple cartoon color treatment. Use the neon tetra and guppy only. Do not include the platy. Do not copy its protruding lips, featherlike fins or guppy crest.
2. Latest two-fish sheet: SECONDARY reference only for the continuous horizontal fish body, obvious gill curve, compact fins, species colors, broad guppy tail and two-column presentation. Do not copy this image's tame eyes, ordinary expressions or polished mascot look.

Make a fresh cohesive sheet with two large fish, one neon tetra and one guppy, plus one small matching repeat underneath each for a thumbnail check. Quiet pale aqua background, plenty of space around every fin. Neon tetra at left facing right; guppy at right facing left. Headings exactly "NEON TETRA" and "GUPPY". No other text.

Character direction:
- Make the eyes the dominant feature. Near eye is a huge protruding off-white globe filling about half the head height, with a small black pupil. Let a smaller far eye peek out above the opposite side of the head. Exaggerate the asymmetry and the slightly bewildered expressions. Big visible whites, not enormous glossy black pupils.
- Neon tetra: bug-eyed, startled, endearingly awkward, an oversized uneven head flowing directly into a short horizontal fish body. Electric cyan stripe, cobalt blue back, red rear belly, pale underside and an obvious forked blue tail. The eyes should be at least as exaggerated as the tetra in reference 1.
- Guppy: eccentric and unimpressed, one enormous cream eye with a heavy half-closed lavender lid and a smaller off-axis far eye. A blunt, slightly lumpy forehead and saggy fish cheek, compact blue/cream body, wide orange fan tail. Keep the funny droopy expression from reference 1.
- Keep small thick fish lips or a tiny pursed O-shaped mouth, but place the mouth flush in the blunt front surface of the head. Mouth points forward horizontally. No protruding paired slabs, pointed snout, projecting upper lip or duckbill. No human teeth or nose.
- Clear curved gill marks immediately behind the heads. The body continues straight from the cheeks to the tail with no neck or throat. Smooth fin membranes, a few sparse fin rays, short side fins attached close to the flanks, small swept-back lower fins, a low simple dorsal fin. The guppy's orange tail is one broad fan-shaped membrane with broad color bands, not separate feathers.

Style: hand-drawn flat 2D cartoon game illustration, old social aquarium game charm, delightfully odd and slightly grotesque but friendly. Bold simple shapes, thin locally colored contours, broad flat color areas and at most one simple cel-shadow. Eyes and eyelids can be lopsided. Keep enough imperfection to feel like comic characters. No heavy black/navy outline, glossy 3D rendering, detailed scales, airbrushed realism or Disney/Pixar polish.

Must avoid: beaks, avian head profiles, feathers, wings, feet, dangling leg-shaped fins, rooster crests, necks, eyelash makeup, realistic small eyes, reducing the eccentric character. Do not solve the bird problem by shrinking the eyes or making the fish anatomically realistic. Preserve the big-eye weirdness; fix only the mouth, body and fin cues.
