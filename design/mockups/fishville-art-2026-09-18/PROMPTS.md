# FishVille-inspired artwork mockup prompts

Created on 18 September 2026 using the built-in Image Gen tool.

These are visual concepts for review. The runtime assets and code are not replaced.

## 01. Starter fish

Reference assets: `assets/species/neonTetra.png`, `assets/species/guppy.png`, `assets/species/platy.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original starter-fish artwork direction board for the aquarium game Fishius, landscape 1792 x 1024.
Primary request: Reimagine the supplied neon tetra, guppy and platy with the appealing flat 2D Flash-cartoon art language of the classic 2009 FishVille aquarium game. This is a visual mockup, not a production sprite atlas. Make a visibly NEW art treatment.
Input images: Image 1 current neon tetra; Image 2 current guppy; Image 3 current platy. Use only for species identity and broad color families. Completely redraw their rendering, shapes, faces and fins in the new style.
Scene/backdrop: flat very pale aqua presentation background, no water texture. Tasteful small dark teal title at top left: "STARTER FISH". Below it small subtitle: "FishVille-inspired artwork study". Three evenly spaced columns, spacious and clear, no borders or cards.
Subjects, left to right:
1. Neon tetra: recognizable slender cyan-blue torpedo body but with a pleasantly rounded forehead, single broad electric cyan stripe from eye toward tail, red stripe on the LOWER REAR HALF only, pale ivory belly at the front, translucent light blue triangular fins, simple forked tail. The long streamlined silhouette must remain distinct from the platy.
2. Guppy: small curved turquoise-blue body, pale cream belly and a wonderfully broad flowing orange and golden fan tail, scalloped tail edge, just 4 or 5 graphic fin rays, small matching orange dorsal fin. Tail is about as tall as the body is long.
3. Platy: short stocky oval apricot-orange body, buttery yellow cheek and belly, small modest rounded red-orange tail, compact dorsal fin. Distinct compact body, not a long-finned goldfish.
Style/medium: authentic clean hand-drawn 2D social-game cartoon. Bold but graceful deep ink-blue outer contour, tapered internal lines, very simple solid color shapes, one soft-edged cel-shadow and one broad restrained highlight at most. Friendly large off-white eye with black pupil and tiny white glint, about one fifth of body height, a little smile and simple cheek curve. Round appealing shapes without making all three the same silhouette. Crisp smooth curves, four to six colors per fish, playful warm expressions. Sparse inner detail that reads at 70 pixels.
Composition: one large fish facing RIGHT in each column, level side profile with full tail and fins. All three at equal visual prominence in the upper-middle two thirds. Species label underneath each, exact text: "Neon tetra", "Guppy", "Platy". Along the lower third, show the EXACT SAME drawing for each fish again at about 90 pixels wide to test game-scale readability, with one shared small label "Small-size preview". Clear visual space around every fish. No other copy.
Constraints: Preserve all three species identities. Original fish designs, no existing FishVille character copies, logos or UI. A cohesive single style shared by all fish. Whole fins and tails visible. No scales, no realistic fish anatomy rendering, no photorealism, no 3D rendering, no plastic shine, no complex gradient airbrushing, no texture, no painterly brushwork, no sparkles, no drop shadows, no badges, no bubbles, no props. The current references are much too glossy and detailed: simplify decisively.
```

## 02. Plants and decor

Reference assets: `assets/decor/catalog/CP-01.png`, `CP-03.png`, `CP-04.png`, `CD-02.png`, `CD-04.png`. River Pebble Pair was described in the prompt.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original plants-and-decor artwork direction board for Fishius, landscape 1792 x 1024.
Primary request: Reimagine six existing aquarium items with the appealing flat 2D Flash-cartoon art language of classic 2009 FishVille. Make a cohesive NEW cartoon art treatment that will match friendly bright fish with deep ink-blue outlines and simple cel shading.
Input images: Images 1-5 are existing catalog assets, in order: Pebble Moss Tuft, Anubias Petite, Java Fern Fan, Spiral Shell, Driftwood Fork. River Pebble Pair is defined by the description below. Reference them only for each object's identity. Redraw and simplify the shapes and shading.
Scene/backdrop: flat very pale aqua presentation background, no texture. Small dark teal title at upper left "PLANTS & DECOR"; small subtitle beneath "FishVille-inspired artwork study". Spacious two-row, three-column arrangement. No boxes or cards. Each object isolated against the shared pale background with plenty of breathing room.
Top row:
1. Pebble Moss Tuft. Three rounded scalloped lime-green moss cushions clustered around one small smooth cool-gray pebble. Simple broad leaf lobes, strong soft silhouette.
2. Anubias Petite. Five broad heart-shaped waxy jade-green leaves on gently curved short stems above a small simplified brown root knot. Each leaf has one curved light midrib, no tiny veins.
3. Java Fern Fan. Five or six tall gently waving lime-and-emerald ribbon leaves spreading from one small rounded rooted base. Supple simplified leaf silhouettes, individual leaves easy to count.
Bottom row:
4. River Pebble Pair. One low wide smooth slate-blue stone with one taller smaller stone behind it. A tiny lime leaf sprout tucked between them. Chunky rounded silhouettes.
5. Spiral Shell. Peach-coral and creamy pale-yellow rounded spiral shell with a coral-pink opening, broad clean spiral line, slightly playful curled lip.
6. Driftwood Fork. Warm honey-brown reclining Y-shaped branch, softly rounded ends with simple growth rings and two flowing dark bark curves. Inviting storybook proportions.
Labels underneath objects, exact text in order: "Pebble Moss Tuft", "Anubias Petite", "Java Fern Fan", "River Pebble Pair", "Spiral Shell", "Driftwood Fork". No other copy.
Style/medium: authentic hand-drawn 2D social-game cartoon, bright cheerful colors, bold graceful deep ink-blue outer contour, tapered sparse internal lines, simple solid color shapes, at most one cel-shadow and one broad subtle highlight. Rounded tactile shapes with slightly exaggerated curls and leaf curves, no realistic material detail. Four to six colors per item. Slight front-above view appropriate for placeable aquarium sprites, coherent ground contact and consistent light from upper left. Clean silhouettes that remain clear at 80 pixels.
Constraints: each object whole and separate, no overlap, no cast shadows beyond a tiny immediate contact oval, no background scene, no tank, no sand, no bubbles, no fish, no UI, no logos, no watermarks. No photorealism, no 3D, no shiny plastic, no brush textures, no speckle, no fine leaf veins or elaborate wood grain. Original artwork inspired by FishVille's simple playful language, not copies of its existing assets.
```

## 03. Tank scene

Reference images: `01-starter-fish.png` and `02-plants-and-decor.png` from this folder.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: complete aquarium scene concept for the native game Fishius, landscape 1792 x 1024.
Primary request: Show how the proposed FishVille-inspired starter fish, plants and decor look together in a playable aquarium scene.
Input images: Image 1 is the NEW starter-fish design board, the strict source of fish drawing style, character faces, species markings and colors. Image 2 is the NEW plants-and-decor board, the strict source of environment item designs. Use the actual designs shown on these boards. Omit all board text and presentation layout.
Scene/backdrop: inviting calm 2D aquarium with clear turquoise-blue water, a light cyan surface near the top, a restrained water gradient toward medium blue at depth, two very subtle broad light rays, distant low blue rock silhouettes, and a warm pale sand bed occupying the lower quarter. Slight downward front view of the sand, natural tank floor with low soft dune curves. Entire picture is underwater with no visible glass frame.
Fish: exactly five fish, all side-view original cartoon sprites with clean bold deep ink-blue contours and flat cel shading matching Image 1. Two slender neon tetras with cyan stripe and red lower rear body, one blue guppy with the very large scalloped orange fan tail, and two compact orange platies with modest red fins. Put the guppy in the middle left facing right, the two neon tetras across the open upper middle at slightly differing depths, and the two platies in the middle right and lower middle. Fish should each be about 9-13 percent of canvas width, guppy up to 16 percent including its tail. Heads, eyes, fins and markings remain legible. Do not enlarge them into enormous foreground characters. They swim separately in ample open water, no overlaps.
Plants and decor: use only the six designs on Image 2. A Java Fern Fan at the left floor edge and another at the right, a compact Anubias Petite and Pebble Moss Tuft near the left bottom, a low reclining Driftwood Fork and one moss tuft near the right bottom, the River Pebble Pair near the back-right sand, and one peach Spiral Shell near the left-middle foreground. These form sparse side clusters with plenty of clear sandy floor across the center. All plants are rooted into the sand. Preserve recognizable silhouettes, bright lime/jade leaves and the shell's coral/cream spiral. Match their outline and shading to the fish.
Style/medium: crisp cheerful flat 2D social-game cartoon, classic FishVille-like charm, sparse detail, rounded playful curves, restrained one-shadow cel shading, no scales, no detailed texture, no plastic shine or 3D materials. Consistent upper-left lighting. The whole scene feels like one artist's work.
Composition: calm, spacious, polished aquarium art test that could sit behind game controls. Keep the upper 12 percent and outer margins fairly open, while still delivering a beautiful standalone full scene. No labels, no text, no title, no logo, no currency, no buttons, no UI, no cards, no grid, no arrows, no frame, no watermarks, no extraneous creatures, no treasure chest, no statues, no coral, no photographic caustics, no gritty sand texture.
```
