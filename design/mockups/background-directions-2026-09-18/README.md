# Background artwork concepts

Three independent concepts generated with built-in Image Gen on 18 September 2026. Their numbering follows the order shown in the conversation.

The user selected the second and third concepts for the game. Their clean background assets are `assets/environment/open-blue-cove-v1.png` and `assets/environment/sage-lagoon-v1.png`. Fish, placed items and controls are rendered separately in the running game.

1. [Violet Rock Grotto](01-violet-rock-grotto.png): plum rock scenery with a broad arch and pale sand.
2. [Open Blue Cove](02-open-blue-cove.png): slate-blue rocks at the sides and a wider clear swimming area.
3. [Sage Lagoon](03-sage-lagoon.png): blue-green rocks, subdued leaf silhouettes and warm sand.

The user found the initial runtime color and softness changes muted but missing FishVille's aesthetic. These concepts redraw the background with broad cartoon shapes, fewer shade planes and simpler sand. The existing game screenshot anchors the interface, six fish and five placed plants and decorations.

The [FishVille screenshot](https://www.mobygames.com/game/52953/fishville/screenshots/browser/525489/) was inspected and attached to every generation as a style reference. Its interface and foreground objects were not requested as replacement game assets.
