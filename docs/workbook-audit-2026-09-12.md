The non-onboarding implementation does not yet fully match the spreadsheet. The imported numbers are consistent with the source, but ordinary play still has blocked progression, missing reward systems, inaccessible seasonal content, and several confirmed defects.

Reviewed on 12 September 2026 against the working tree, including uncommitted files. The source is [aquarium_game_master_model.xlsx](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/design/aquarium_game_master_model.xlsx), SHA-256 `1b6fe414def144f7792f998a779ac027ac5fe6af6edb8d17380d6933f3c4e77c`. The earlier 11 September audit covers a different, 17-sheet workbook. Its decor findings are stale. The current code implements the new 120-item catalog, first-ownership XP, distinct placed-item score, storage, transfers, and animation limits.

**Findings, in priority order**

1. **High: A paid egg can block the player's care and selling controls.** Start with the four starter fish, buy and place six Neon Tetras, then buy a seventh. The tank contains ten fish and one paid egg is queued. Purchases deliberately skip capacity checks. Every subsequent water tap tries to place that egg before checking the selected tool. Select, Food, and Sell therefore all fail with the full-tank error. Done does not suspend the queue, and saving preserves it. A player with only the starter tank cannot sell a fish or feed the tank to recover. The independent UI probe reproduced all three blocked actions. This is a code defect, independent of missing spreadsheet rules. See [purchase capacity handling](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/domain.cpp:174), [water-tap routing](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/view.cpp:159), and [tool changes](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/view.cpp:26). Keep the paid egg available while allowing the player to leave placement mode and use care tools.

2. **High: Cancelling decor placement destroys a paid copy.** `Decor Guide!C19` says every purchase creates one permanent cosmetic copy. A queued purchase currently charges immediately but only sets `pendingDecor`. Escape or a tool change clears that string without creating a stored copy or refunding the cost. A probe bought PP-01 for two Pearls, cancelled, and reloaded. It still had two fewer Pearls and zero copies. Lifetime ownership remained recorded, so buying again would also give no first-purchase XP. See [purchase and cancellation](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/decor.cpp:72) and [tool cancellation](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/view.cpp:27). Create the permanent copy at purchase and keep it in inventory when placement is cancelled. This behavior is deliberately documented and tested in the current project, but it conflicts with the workbook.

3. **Resolved: Tank capacity now uses Pearls.** `Tanks & Social!G4:H8` provides Pearl and Coin costs. Each expansion and new tank accepts either listed price. The user clarified on 12 September that Coins and Pearls are alternative payments; the selected price button charges only its currency. Players can earn Pearls from level rewards or open the Pearl shop when short.

4. **High: An early player can lose every route back into the fish economy.** Without using onboarding actions, buy three Molly and one Neon Tetra, spending 247 coins. Spend the earned Level 2 Pearl on Bubble Eye. After seven days without feeding, the probe had nine dead fish, three coins, zero Pearls, and no buyable species or affordable revival. Gifts and the Basket cannot rescue that Level 2 state. `Currency Matrix!E4` requires protection from bankruptcy in the first three sessions. This sequence demonstrates an unprotected early return, not a measured three-session playtest. See [care and death](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/domain.cpp:70), [sale eligibility](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/domain.cpp:68), and [revival price](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/domain.cpp:214). A rescue policy, amount, eligibility rule, and repeat-claim limit are unspecified.

5. **High when storage fails: The player is not told that progress is unsaved.** `Session::command` returns gameplay success even when its checkpoint fails. The error goes into `Session::status()`, which has no application UI reader. A probe used a save path with a regular file as its parent. The purchase succeeded and changed the session, while the save failed silently from the player's perspective. Recovery notices are hidden by the same path. See [command checkpoint and save status](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/storage.cpp:117) and [view command feedback](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/view.cpp:17). Surface persistent save failures and recovery notices. This is a general code defect, not an explicit workbook rule.

6. **Medium: Most premium content cannot be acquired in normal play.** The only available Pearl grant is one Pearl at Level 2. Later grants, events, offers, and IAP listed in `Currency Matrix!C5:D5` are absent. Currency-shop packs are inactive previews. The other nine premium fish cost 3–20 Pearls, and the 22 launch evergreen Pearl decor items start at two Pearls. None can be funded from the one-Pearl lifetime earning path. See [level grant](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/domain.cpp:104) and [inactive packs](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/currency_shop.cpp:96). The workbook does not supply a complete grant schedule or store product configuration.

7. **Medium: The Shop excludes all limited fish and all seasonal decor, even during valid events.** `Quests & Live Ops!C13` requires the limited fish calendar to affect availability. `Decor Guide!C35` and `Decor Live Ops!C5:H16` provide twelve seasonal Coin/Pearl pairs. The Shop filters out every LE fish and every Limited Edition decor item without checking dates. The probe confirmed that Heartfin was eligible on February 10 with sufficient level and coins, but all fish Shop pages contained only 36 regular species. A configured Winterfest also made PL-11 eligible in the domain while the Shop still excluded it. See [both filters](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/skin.cpp:27). A calendar alone will not fix this. The UI needs an event purchase route. Keeping unspecified decor dates closed is correct; permanently filtering configured events is the separate mismatch.

   | System | Workbook requirement | Current behavior |

The newer Decor Guide describes potential cosmetic uses and future set badges, so those also need scope confirmation. |

   See [quest import](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/tools/import_supplement.py:35), [quest rewards and claims](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/domain.cpp:261), [mastery claim](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/domain.cpp:245), and [premature decor progress](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/decor.cpp:99). Do not give every daily quest the entire coin pool when defining the missing split.

9. **Medium: Several live-ops and social systems exist only as design data or unavailable actions.** The Daily Egg Basket at `Quests & Live Ops!C12:F12`, event eggs and gift chains at `Currency Matrix!C10:E10`, and token-based social fish/crafting at `Currency Matrix!D8` have no complete player flow. The Basket always returns unavailable after its Level 6 gate. See [Basket action](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/domain.cpp:284). Normal purchased eggs implement hatching, but do not implement random daily rewards, retired-fish tables, a weekly rotation, or crafting. Drop tables, recipes, resale rules, and limits need specification.

10. **Medium: Decor can be placed entirely outside the visible area.** The validator accepts a bottom-centre anchor at y=0 while the whole sprite is drawn above that anchor and clipped to the screen. A CP-01 probe at `(544,0)` succeeded and added ten score, but its visible rectangle ended at y=0. It remained a placed copy and used the placement budget. This conflicts with the footprint and usable-anchor requirements in `Decor Guide!C45`, `C49`, and `C62`. See [anchor-only validation](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/decor.cpp:53), [sprite rectangle](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/decor_canvas.cpp:69), and [clipping](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/canvas.cpp:269). Validate the visible footprint for purchases, moves, and restores, or retain an accessible recovery handle.

11. **Medium: The decor layer rules and interaction mode differ from the spreadsheet.** `Decor Guide!C50` says to use each item's Foreground/Midground/Background layer and restrict decor hit-testing to decorate mode. The renderer instead sorts everything by bottom-anchor y and draws all decor behind fish. Normal Select mode also allows decor movement and stashing. Fish retain selection priority, so the latter is not itself a confirmed obstruction. See [render order](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/canvas.cpp:260) and [normal selection](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/selection.cpp:8). Both choices are deliberate in current docs and tests. Decide which interaction/rendering contract should govern, then align the spreadsheet and implementation.

12. **Medium: Reduced motion and restored settings are misleading in the UI.** `Decor Guide!C58` calls for a user-controlled reduced-motion switch. The still rendering works, but the only UI setter is Graphics Low. Medium and High disable it. The UI also initializes its sound slider and quality selection independently of saved values. A saved 20% volume and reduced motion reopened as 80% and High, despite the domain retaining the saved settings. Music controls change state or a local slider without any implemented music playback. See [independent view defaults](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/include/aquarium/view.hpp:121), [view initialization](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/view.cpp:11), and `View::settings` in [skin.cpp](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/skin.cpp). Expose reduced motion clearly and initialize controls from persisted state. Notifications tied to onboarding were excluded from this audit.

13. **Lower priority: Concrete premium visual promises are not implemented.** `Premium Fish!X3` specifies unique Koi patterns per fish and `X7` specifies timed Flashlight Fish glow. `fishArt` ignores individual fish identity and returns one species texture. Fish rendering applies shared mesh movement and care tinting, without either species-specific feature. See [art selection](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/skin.cpp:66) and [fish rendering](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/src/canvas.cpp:246). Descriptive references to prestige, happiness, or breeding elsewhere are not enough to infer entire additional systems.

14. **Balance remains unverified because intended sources and sinks are missing.** `Currency Matrix!C4:D4`, `Overview!B15`, and `Inputs!B83` mention daily bonuses, friend visits, achievement/collection grants, paid basic food, recurring maintenance, and crafting. Food is free; the recurring systems are absent. Fish sales, fish purchases, decor purchases, and the configured tank charges work. The missing amounts and cadence need design decisions. Adding costs before fixing recovery and material earning could make the blocked progression worse.

15. **High in the later tested UI snapshot: New Shop asset names do not match the files.** Four test suites fail while opening/preparing Shop because the renderer requests `assets/shop/reference-tab-0.png`. The generated files use names such as `reference-tab-fish.png` and `reference-tab-plants.png`. At the time of the check there was no numbered tab file. See the retained [Shop source snapshot](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/evidence/workbook-audit-2026-09-12/skin-final-review.cpp:394) and [later test log](/Users/amoghrijal/amogh/aquarium-native/evidence/workbook-audit-2026-09-12/ctest-final.log). Startup prepares the Shop, so this path can prevent normal launch. This finding arose during concurrent UI work and should be rechecked when that work settles.

**Source decisions and workbook issues**

- **Bubble Eye purchase policy:** `Premium Fish!AA2` explicitly says repeatable. `Unlock Plan!F5`, `Inputs!F111`, `XP Curve!H5`, and `Balance Review!B6` say one-time. The importer deliberately follows the detailed repeatable rule. This affects later purchases even though some conflicting text mentions the tutorial.
- **Anniversary Rainbowfish:** `Limited Edition!D11` says one week yearly without dates. `Q11/S11` assigns 250/63 sale XP while `Y11/Z11` prohibits resale. The runtime honors non-resellable status. A separate maturity reward should not be invented to pay those numbers.
- **Expansion gates:** `Unlock Plan!G7/G13/G16/G22/G25/G31/G34/G40/G43` lists Medium/Large beats, while `Tanks & Social!E4:E8` provides four sizes, 10/20/30/40. The importer records an unconfirmed mapping. With supplied funds/tokens, current code permits all Tank 1 expansions at Level 1. Resolve the mapping before claiming level-gated expansion parity.
- **Feed Caretaker wording:** One fish fed eight times completes the objective. `Quests & Live Ops!C4` says eight fish but does not expressly say eight distinct IDs. Confirm the intended meaning instead of silently changing the target.
- **Incomplete reward definitions:** Daily coin splits, token grants/limits, weekly membership and rewards, mastery XP/statues, Decorator thresholds, Basket tables, crates, and rescue rules lack enough detail for exact implementation. These are identifiable gaps, not a reason to describe the systems as finished.
- **Weak PASS checks:** `Balance Review!F5` only checks whether any Coin Fish stage-XP cell is numeric. `F10/F11` are literal `="PASS"`. `F7/F17` checks Heartfin below 150 coins/hour rather than the comparison promised by `Dashboard!C23`. Current catalog checks pass independently, but those workbook checks would miss future errors.
- **Hardcoded dashboard cost:** `Dashboard!B25` embeds `110000+220000+380000+600000` rather than reading Tank 5's cost schedule. Its current 20.7-day result matches, but repricing `Tanks & Social!H8` will not update that numerator or its dependent PASS check.
- **Forecast boundary:** `Economy Simulation!C4:D73` assumes capacity from level unlocks, and `G4:H73` applies theoretical frontier rates and utilization. Its roughly 251-day Level 70 result is a directional model. It does not establish actual progression when tokens are unavailable. Decor spend and one-time XP are explicitly excluded by `Overview!B26` and `Decor Guide!C31`; that exclusion is not an implementation defect.

Levels 41–70, Tanks 6–8, the 53 Roadmap fish, and 24 evergreen decor entries above Level 40 were not counted as missing launch features. All seasonal items were assessed separately from that future scope. Complete-set decor badges are expressly future/conditional in `Decor Guide!C38`.
